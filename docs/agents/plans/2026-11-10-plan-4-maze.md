# Plan 4 — Maze Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Add the Maze minigame. A maze with walls and spikes is shown for a few seconds and then hidden. The player types the whole route (WASD or 1234), and the route is simulated and replayed.

**Architecture:** Follows the add-minigame skill. Pure logic (generation, BFS, route parsing and simulation, level table) is in `src/games/maze/logic.ts`. There are two presentational components, `MazeGrid.vue` (cells plus a path overlay) and `RouteInput.vue` (the filtered input, arrows and pad), and `MazeGame.vue` runs the round. The level persists through the stats store staircase. The key set comes from the global `useSettingsStore().mazeKeys`.

**Spec:** §6.4. Rulings: `docs/agents/questions/006` (round flow, the 300 ms guard) and `008` (everything below).

## Global Constraints

- Plan 1's Global Constraints and `CLAUDE.md` apply (`useGameKeys`; unmount wrappers in component tests; lint before each commit).
- Branch `feat/maze` from `feat/plates`. Push when Task 4 is green.
- **Grid:** odd size N. Coordinates `(x, y)`, with `cells[y][x]`. Rooms are at **even, even** coordinates. Cells with one odd coordinate are walls between two rooms, and odd, odd cells are always walls. **There is no outer wall ring**: stepping off the grid is the edge. Start is `(0, 0)` and end is `(N−1, N−1)`.
- **Generation:** a randomized depth-first carve over the rooms, then `EXTRA_OPENING_RATE = 0.1` of the remaining between-room walls are opened. **Spikes** go on open cells that aren't start, end or a neighbour of start. Candidates are shuffled; a spike is kept only if BFS from start to end still succeeds. This always terminates and may place fewer spikes than requested.
- **Level table**, staircase `{ start: 1, min: 1, max: 10, upAfter: 3, upStep: 1, downAfter: 1, downStep: 1 }`:

  | L | N | spikes | view s |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | 5 | 0 | 6 |
  | 2 | 5 | 1 | 5 |
  | 3 | 7 | 1 | 7 |
  | 4 | 7 | 2 | 6 |
  | 5 | 9 | 2 | 8 |
  | 6 | 9 | 3 | 7 |
  | 7 | 11 | 3 | 9 |
  | 8 | 11 | 4 | 8 |
  | 9 | 13 | 5 | 10 |
  | 10 | 13 | 6 | 9 |

  The setting `timeMultiplier` runs 0.5–2 (step 0.25, default 1), and `viewMs = max(1000, round(view·1000·mult))`.
- **Keys:** WASD means `w` up, `a` left, `s` down, `d` right. 1234 means `1` up, `2` left, `3` down, `4` right. Case-insensitive; other characters are ignored; there are no arrow-key aliases. Use `inputmode="numeric"` for 1234 and `"text"` for WASD.
- **Simulation:** stepping into a wall, onto a spike or off the grid **loses**. Reaching the end **wins**, and moves after the end are ignored. Ending anywhere else loses (`short`). **Shortest** = moves up to the end equal the BFS distance; it is recorded as `metrics.shortest` 1/0 and shown, and it doesn't change the level.
- **Input UI:** one text input that filters to the key set. Each move shows as an arrow `↑ ← ↓ →`, Backspace works, and Enter submits. A pad (↑ ← ↓ → ⌫ ✓) is always shown and uses `@pointerdown.prevent`, so it never steals focus. No recall time limit.
- **Replay:** after submitting, the maze reappears with the path animated at ~120 ms per step (instant with `prefers-reduced-motion`). It stops at the failing cell, which flashes red. Next works during the replay (with the 300 ms / no-repeat Enter guard). A **"Show a shortest route"** button draws a BFS path.

## Review Focus

1. **Every generated maze is solvable**, including after spikes, across many seeds and every level. Pinned in Task 1 (`500 seeds × 10 levels solvable`).
2. **Messy route text** (spaces, uppercase, letters from the other key set, emoji) is filtered, not crashed on. Pinned in Task 1 (`parseRoute`).
3. **Falling off the grid edge** (e.g. `w` from the start) loses with outcome `outside`. Pinned in Task 1.
4. **Pad buttons don't steal focus or double-submit**, and Enter in the route input submits exactly once. Pinned in Task 3.
5. **Quitting during the replay** leaves no running timers or errors. Pinned in Task 3 (`unmount during replay`).

### Task 1: Maze logic

**Interfaces (Produces):**
- `MAZE_ID = 'maze'`; `type Cell = 'open' | 'wall' | 'spike'`; `interface Pos { x: number; y: number }`; `type Dir = 'up' | 'left' | 'down' | 'right'`

- `interface MazeSettings extends GameSettings { timeMultiplier: number }`; `MAZE_DEFAULTS = { timeMultiplier: 1 }`; `MAZE_FIELDS`
- `interface MazeLevel { size: number; spikes: number; viewSeconds: number }`; `MAZE_LEVELS` (the table); `MAZE_STAIRCASE`; `mazeLevelInfo(level)` (clamped); `mazeViewMs(level, mult)`
- `summarizeMaze(rounds)`: headline `largest <N>×<N>` (the max `metrics.size` over wins), `'no maze solved yet'`, or `'not played'`. Stats: `Rounds`, `Largest solved`, `Win rate (last 20)`, `Shortest routes` (`<k> of <wins>`).
